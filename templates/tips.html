General Sleep Tips
Consistent Schedule

Go to bed and wake up at the same time every day, even on weekends.

Cool Room

Keep your bedroom at 60-67°F (15-19°C) for optimal sleep.

No Screens

Avoid screens 1-2 hours before bed. Use blue light filters if needed.

Limit Alcohol

Avoid alcohol close to bedtime as it disrupts sleep quality.
